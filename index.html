Brickwell

How many bricks or blocks to buy for a wall, and the mortar to go with them. Put in the wall size and how it's built, and it works out the units, the sand and cement, and draws the courses to scale.

The wall

A course of bricks is 75 mm tall, a course of blocks 225 mm, so ten brick courses is about 750 mm if you're guessing the height.

How it's built

A half-brick wall is one brick thick, the usual garden or single-skin wall. A one-brick wall is two skins. A standard block is 440 × 215 mm.

Allowances (optional)

Add up the doors and windows into one square-metre figure and Brickwell takes them off the wall. The spare margin covers cuts and breakages; 5% is usual.

Planning figures to the common UK merchant and trade guidance (Travis Perkins, Brickhunter, Engineering ToolBox) and the standard metric brick (215 × 102.5 × 65 mm) and block (440 × 215 mm) sizes. A buying guide only; a structural or retaining wall needs an engineer's design. Runs entirely in your browser.
